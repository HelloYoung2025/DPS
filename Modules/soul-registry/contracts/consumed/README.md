# Consumed contracts

Schemas consumed from another owner are referenced through ../module.yaml. Do not fork or locally weaken an upstream schema.

## First-slice dependency boundary

The identity owner assets introduce no consumed contract, source-project dependency or copied Binding/Host/Policy/Ledger implementation. Caller enums describe authenticated roles expected by a future provider; they do not authorize those callers. Soul proves identity, while Binding continues to own the device/account tuple and mutation fence.
